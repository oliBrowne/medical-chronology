## Facts of Incident

On March 14, 2024 at approximately 5:40 PM, Client was stopped at a red light westbound on Highway 36 at Fairview Pkwy when her 2017 Honda Civic was rear-ended by a box truck owned by Holt Logistics, LLC. The truck driver was cited for following too closely. Client was the belted driver; airbags did not deploy.

Client was taken by ambulance to Front Range Regional Medical Center that evening.

## Claimed Injuries

1. Cervical strain / cervical disc injury (C5-6)
2. Lumbar strain with ongoing low back pain
3. Right shoulder rotator cuff tear

## Prior Medical History

Client denies any prior neck or back problems. Client reports she was "completely healthy" before the collision and had never seen a chiropractor or physical therapist.
