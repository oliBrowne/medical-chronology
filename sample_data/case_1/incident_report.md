## Narrative

Unit 2 was stopped westbound on Hwy 36 at the red signal at Fairview Pkwy. Unit 1 was traveling westbound behind Unit 2 and failed to stop, striking the rear of Unit 2. Driver 1 stated he looked down at his dispatch tablet and "didn't see the light change." Driver 1 estimated his speed at 20-25 mph at time of impact. Skid marks measured approx. 14 ft.

Driver 2 stated she was "jolted forward and back" and hit the back of her head on the headrest. Driver 2 complained of neck pain and pain to her left shoulder. Driver 2 denied loss of consciousness. Fairview Fire Rescue Medic 4 arrived at 17:51 and evaluated Driver 2 on scene. Driver 2 declined transport and stated she would have a friend drive her to the hospital.

Unit 2 was drivable. Unit 1 was drivable.
