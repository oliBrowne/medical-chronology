## Facts of Incident

On May 9, 2024 at approximately 2:15 PM, Client slipped on a wet floor in the produce aisle of Harbor Fresh Market, 410 Mill St, Fairview, CO. No wet-floor sign was posted. Client fell and landed on his outstretched left hand.

Client was taken by ambulance to Fairview Urgent Care that afternoon.

## Claimed Injuries

1. Left distal radius fracture
2. Lumbar strain with ongoing low back pain

## Prior Medical History

Client denies any prior back problems. Client reports he had never been treated for back pain before the fall.
